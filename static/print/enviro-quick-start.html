ENVIRO + 1NCE CELLULAR
QUICK START

Your first sensor data.

From your Enviro device to insigh.io Console.

Firmware and SIM are ready. Have a WiFi-capable phone or computer, internet access and your sensor specifications to hand.

1 Prepare the hardware

With power off, connect sensors through the glands to the Enviro ports. Follow the hardware guide for wiring and power-mode jumpers.

Check the sensor supply voltage before powering on: all sensor power ports share the selected 5V / 9V / 12V supply.

Important: analogue voltage inputs accept 0-3.3V; 4-20mA sensors need the corresponding current-mode jumper. Missing power-mode jumpers leave sensors unpowered.

Wiring diagrams: full guide → Hardware Setup.

2 Create your device in Console

  1. Open console.insigh.io. Sign up, verify your email, then log in.
  2. Open Device List, press +, enter a device name and select Create.
  3. On the device page, open More Details (...) and copy the access details using the icon next to JSON.

Keep the JSON ready for step 4; keep these keys private. Do this before joining the device's WiFi.

3 Connect to the device

Power on. When the LED blinks magenta, connect to the device's WiFi within 50 seconds.

WiFi name
insigh-XXXX (varies by device)
WiFi password
insighiodev
Browser address
http://192.168.4.1
Login
admin / insighiodev

Stay on this WiFi even with no internet. Solid magenta means connected. Complete setup within 5 minutes of connecting. If setup closes, power off/on and reconnect.

CONFIGURE & VERIFY

Connect. Measure. Check.

4 Configure your device

Network: Cellular
APN: iot.1nce.net | Technology: NB-IoT
Transport: MQTT | IP: IPv4

Console keys: paste your JSON into ID. ID, Key, Data Channel and Control Channel fill automatically.

Measurements: enable board readings such as battery. Select SDI-12/Modbus/ADC for Enviro and configure only your connected sensors:

  • SDI-12: address, command and warm-up time.
  • Modbus: serial settings, sensor address, register and data format.
  • Analogue / pulse: enable the wired inputs. Match analogue gain to the signal range.

Use your sensors' specifications. Keep advanced system settings at their defaults.

Timing: for testing, set the sleep / measurement period to 5 minutes (300s); Batch Upload off.

5 Apply and check your data

  1. Review and press Finish. The device reboots, measures and attempts an upload.
  2. Reconnect to the internet. Open your device in console.insigh.io.
  3. Check for a new timestamp in Live View and readings in Active Measurements.

Normal LED cycle: blue = measuring; red = connecting; green = uploading; off = sleeping.
Confirm success in Console, not by LED colour alone.

No connection or readings?

No WiFi? Power off/on; repeat step 3.
No uploads? Check coverage, APN and keys. Unsure of NB-IoT coverage? Try technology auto.
No sensor values? Check sensor settings. Power off before checking wiring or power jumpers.
