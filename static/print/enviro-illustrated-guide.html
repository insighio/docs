01 / Prepare

Start with your device.

Your firmware is installed and the SIM is fitted. Next: connect your sensors, create a device in Console, and configure your first upload.

Before powering on

  • With power off, route sensor cables through the glands and connect them to the matching Enviro ports.
  • Check sensor wiring and power-mode jumpers against the hardware guide. Missing power-mode jumpers leave sensors unpowered.
  • Match the 5V / 9V / 12V supply to your sensors. All sensor power ports share this selected voltage.

Protect your inputs: analogue voltage inputs accept 0-3.3V. A 4-20mA sensor needs the corresponding current-mode jumper. Do not confuse sensor supply voltage with input range.

Have ready: a WiFi-capable phone or computer with a browser, internet access for Console, and your sensor specifications.

Wiring and jumpers: open the full guide below and choose Hardware Setup. This insert does not replace those diagrams.

02 / Register

Give your device a home.

Console is where your measurements will appear. Create its device record while your phone or computer still has internet access.

Create your account and device

  1. Visit console.insigh.io. Sign up and verify your email, then log in. Already registered? Just log in.
  2. Open Device List and press +.
  3. Enter a distinct device name, then press Create. The other fields are optional.

Copy the access keys

  1. On the new device's Device Info page, open More Details (...).
  2. Use the copy icon next to JSON to copy the ID, Key, Data Channel and Control Channel together.

Keep the JSON ready for page 3. These keys identify your device to Console. Keep them private; do not share screenshots or printouts containing them.

Leave the device page open for the final check. In the next step you will temporarily leave internet WiFi to connect directly to the node.

03 / Connect

Open local setup.

Power on and wait for the LED to blink magenta (around 5-10 seconds). Join its WiFi within 50 seconds.

WiFi name
insigh-XXXX (varies by device)
WiFi password
insighiodev
Open in browser
http://192.168.4.1
Login
admin / insighiodev

Stay connected even with no internet. Solid magenta means connected. Finish within 5 minutes of connecting; if setup closes, power off/on and reconnect.

Network: Cellular
APN: iot.1nce.net | Technology: NB-IoT
Transport: MQTT | IP: IPv4

Keys: paste the JSON from Console into ID. ID, Key, Data Channel and Control Channel fill automatically.

Measurements: enable battery readings; select SDI-12/Modbus/ADC for Enviro. Configure only connected sensors, using their specifications:

  • SDI-12: address, command and warm-up time.
  • Modbus: serial settings, address, register and data format.
  • Analogue / pulse: wired inputs; match analogue gain to signal range.

04 / Verify

See your first readings.

Timing: for a first test, set the sleep / measurement period to 5 minutes (300s) and Batch Upload off. Keep advanced system settings at their defaults.

Review and press Finish. The device applies the settings, reboots, measures and attempts an upload.

Confirm in Console

  1. Reconnect your phone or computer to the internet, then open your device at console.insigh.io.
  2. Look for a new timestamp in Live View. Use > to expand the measurement.
  3. Check the latest readings in Active Measurements. Refresh the page to update device status.

Success means data in Console. LED colours show stages, not upload success. An unlit LED during sleep is normal.

Need a quick check?

No setup WiFi? Power off/on and repeat page 3.
No uploads? Check cellular coverage, APN and Console keys. Unsure of NB-IoT availability? Try technology preference auto.
Missing sensor values? Check sensor settings; power off before checking wiring or power jumpers.
